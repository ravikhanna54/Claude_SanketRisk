Platform & Getting Started
What is SanketRisk ONE?+
A unified commercial property risk intelligence platform covering the full inspection lifecycle — assignment, field data collection, AI-assisted analysis and recommendations, quantitative risk grading, QA review, and report generation — for loss control consultants, inspection firms, and insurance underwriters.
Who is the platform built for?+
Field inspectors performing full manual COPE inspections, QA reviewers approving submitted reports, underwriters relying on the finished grades and narratives, and executives using the analytics layer to review portfolio-wide trends. Access to each area is controlled separately by account role.
Where does inspection data live, and how is it secured?+
All data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database with Row Level Security enforced at the database level — not just hidden in the interface. Role-based access separates admin, inspector, and executive analytics accounts, and every account’s visibility is checked fresh on each request, not just cached at login.
Is there a mobile-friendly way to submit inspection requests?+
Yes — a public-facing Inspection Request Portal lets brokers, insureds, or property managers submit a request directly. Requests appear on the admin dashboard for assignment, and convert directly into a pre-populated inspection form once assigned — no re-keying of the requestor’s details.
Inspections & Reporting
What does a full inspection cover?+
A structured COPE framework — Construction, Occupancy, Protection, and Exposure — along with Natural Catastrophe, Loss History, and Safety sections. A library of 35+ specialist supplement modules (restaurant, warehouse, woodworking, auto service, sprinkler systems, and more) activate automatically or manually based on the occupancy.
What report formats can I generate?+
Three, all generated from the same underlying field data: a Screen Print view for quick reference, a Formal Structured Report with full field-by-field detail, and an AI-generated Narrative Report that turns the same data into readable prose. All three include a cover page and the risk scores.
How does the automatic recommendation engine work? Live+
Specific field entries — a sprinkler condition marked "Poor," a service tag marked missing, heavy grease buildup on a kitchen exhaust filter — automatically generate a prioritized, NFPA-referenced recommendation with no manual drafting. Each recommendation carries a defined priority level (Immediate, 30-Day, Advisory) tied directly to what was observed, so the same observation always produces the same recommendation across every inspector.
What does the AI Pre-Review actually check for? Live+
Before a report reaches QA, an AI pass reviews the completed form for internal inconsistencies — for example, a deficiency described in a recommendation but not reflected in the related score, or a field left blank where a related observation implies it shouldn’t be. This is the kind of gap a senior consultant’s review would normally catch, surfaced automatically before submission rather than after.
How does QA review work?+
A submitted report moves through a structured pipeline — submit, assign to a reviewer, approve or return with feedback — with a full audit trail preserved at every step. Reviewers see the same AI Pre-Review flags the inspector saw, plus the complete field history.
Can I bring in existing legacy inspection files? Live+
Yes — the File Archive lets you upload existing Word or PDF inspection reports and convert them into SanketRisk ONE’s standard structured format, including COPE scores generated from the document’s own content. This brings years of archived inspections into one searchable, gradeable system rather than leaving them as static files.
Risk Grading
How many separate grading systems does a report carry?+
Three, independently calculated: a COPE-based risk grade (Construction, Protection, Safety, and an overall composite score), a five-tier Sprinkler System grade based on SRC demerit methodology and NFPA 25 deficiency classification, and a Natural Catastrophe grade covering earthquake, wind/storm, and flood/water exposure. Each is fully traceable back to its component factors in the Formal Report.
Is the grading actually consistent between inspectors?+
Every demerit value is fixed and documented — the same observation always carries the same point weight, regardless of which inspector recorded it. This is deliberately not left to individual judgment on the numbers themselves; the judgment call is choosing which option best matches what was observed, and the reference guide is built specifically to standardize that choice.
Nat-Cat & Geographic Data
Does Nat-Cat grading work for both Canada and the U.S.? Live+
Yes. Canadian properties reference the National Building Code of Canada 2025, Appendix C. U.S. properties use live data from the U.S. Geological Survey (seismic), FEMA (flood zone), and NOAA (rainfall) — queried fresh for the specific address at the time of the report, not pulled from a static table. This means U.S. coverage works immediately for any address, without waiting for a particular region’s data to be pre-loaded.
What is the building footprint verification feature? Live+
An independent cross-check of the declared building area against mapped building outline data, sourced from Microsoft’s building footprint datasets. This is loaded region-by-region for both Canada and the U.S. and expands over time; where a region isn’t yet loaded, the platform falls back to other available sources rather than showing nothing.
What is the hydrant location feature, and how reliable is it? Live+
Nearby fire hydrant locations surface directly within SCAN ONE assessments, sourced from OpenStreetMap. This is genuinely useful as a convenience cross-check, but it’s crowdsourced data — coverage varies significantly by area, and a "no hydrants found" result means none have been mapped nearby, not that none exist. It’s presented as a best-effort reference, not an authoritative source, and the platform says so directly wherever it’s shown.
SCAN ONE & AI Analytics
What is Sanket SCAN ONE? Live+
A photo-based AI property assessment. The insured, broker, or property manager submits photos via a simple upload link; the AI analyzes them to identify construction class, building characteristics, occupancy indicators, hazards, and exposure factors, then generates a structured report in the standard SanketRisk ONE format — without a physical site visit. Clearly delivered as a Photo-Based Preliminary Assessment, suited to renewals, lower-value properties, and high-volume preliminary screening.
What is the AI Analytics dashboard? Live+
A natural-language query tool over your own portfolio data — ask a plain-English question and get back both an AI-generated summary and the actual underlying data it was drawn from, side by side. It's restricted to specifically authorized executive-level accounts, kept separate from ordinary admin access, and every query runs read-only against a fixed, curated set of data views — it can never write to or alter your data.
Roadmap
Can reports carry my own company’s branding? Roadmap+
Not yet — every report currently carries SanketRisk branding. White-label report delivery, showing a client’s own logo and name in place of ours, is on the roadmap as part of the planned Carrier Integration & White-Label Delivery product.
Can I export a report directly to ACORD format, or push it into my PAS? Roadmap+
Not yet — both ACORD-standard form export and direct Policy Administration System (PAS) API integration are planned, not currently available. See the Services & Roadmap section for the current state of this work.
Is building replacement cost valuation available? Partially Live+
Sanket VALUATION ONE currently delivers RSMeans-based replacement cost estimates as a consultant-assisted service — not yet a fully self-serve, automated feature. A direct API-integrated valuation provider for full self-serve automation is under evaluation as a future upgrade.
