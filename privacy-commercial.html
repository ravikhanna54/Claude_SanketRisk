Privacy Policy

SanketRisk ONE, operated by Sanket Risk Consultants. Last updated: October 2026.

What this service is

SanketRisk ONE is a private inspection and reporting platform used by Sanket Risk Consultants, its inspectors, reviewers and authorised clients. Access is by invitation only.

Information we collect

Account details (name, email, role), inspection and property information entered by users, photos and documents uploaded to inspections, and basic technical logs needed to keep the service secure.

How we use it

To prepare, review and deliver inspection reports, to operate and secure the platform, and to keep encrypted backups. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the client or insurer the inspection was performed for, and with service providers that host and secure the platform (for example cloud hosting, database storage, email delivery and backup storage). Where reports use AI assistance, the relevant inspection text may be processed by an AI service provider for that purpose only.

Backups and Google Drive

Encrypted backups of our own data are stored in a Google Drive account owned by Sanket Risk Consultants. The backup tool accesses only that account and no one else's Google data.

Security and retention

Data is protected with access controls and encryption in transit and at rest. Records are kept as long as needed for the inspection purpose and legal obligations.

Your choices

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact us at ravi@sanketrisk.com.
